# 🎵 Looper Player v1.0.0-beta.1 (Desktop Release)

We are thrilled to announce the first official beta release of **Looper Player**—completely re-architected from the ground up as a high-fidelity, native desktop music player for **Windows** and **Linux**.

Looper pairs an ultra-performant, zero-telemetry local audio engine with an adaptive glassmorphic cyber-interface that dynamically morphs with whatever album you're listening to.

---

## ✨ Key Highlights

### 🔮 Glassmorphic Cyber-UI & Adaptive Theming (Default ON)
* **Album-Art Adaptive Color Extraction**: The entire interface—navigation rails, seekbars, text glows, and backdrop blurs—adapts dynamically in real time to the dominant palette of active artwork.
* **Deep Neo-Blur Aesthetics**: Subtle frosted acrylic glassmorphism and real-time backdrop blur enabled out of the box, with an **OLED Pure Black** mode for deep contrast and AMOLED displays.

### 🎤 Syllable & Karaoke-Glow Lyrics
* **Word-by-Word Illumination**: Rich, word-level glowing karaoke lyrics engine with butter-smooth scrolling.
* **Interactive Seeking & Calibration**: Tap any lyric line to skip straight to that timestamp, paired with a per-song calibration tool to shift timing offsets.
* **Multi-Provider Fallback**: Automatically resolves embedded tags, local `.lrc` files, and fallback providers.

### 🪟 Dual Floating Mini-Players & Real-Time Visualizer
* **Standard Floating Mini-Player**: Resizable card with cover art, playback controls, seekbar, and frequency spectrum.
* **Pinned Corner Desktop Widget**: Ultra-compact, non-intrusive floating corner player featuring a real-time reactive audio frequency visualizer.
* **48-Band DSP Spectrum Visualizer**: Hardware-accelerated audio frequency bars reacting directly to live audio output in both the full player deck and mini-players (auto-pauses to preserve CPU when idle).

### 📊 Looper Analyze (Listening Trends & Stats)
* Track your listening habits locally without giving away your personal data: total hours listened, 30-day play trends, top tracks, favorite artists, listening streaks, and genre breakdowns.

### 🔋 Intelligent Auto Battery Saver
* Senses when your laptop is unplugged and actively discharging: automatically engages battery-preserving overrides (freezes continuous animations, suspends GPU blur filters, and switches to OLED pure black) when power drops below your chosen threshold.

### 🎵 18-Band Graphical EQ & Audio Controls
* **Interactive Drag-to-Shape Curve**: Flexible 18-band equalizer (65 Hz to 20 kHz) with ±20 dB range, zero-snapping logic, and built-in genre presets.
* **Expressive & Flat Seekbars**: Choose between an organic, fluid animating wave seekbar or a clean, CPU-efficient flat progress bar.

### 🎧 Scrobbling & System Integrations
* **Last.fm & ListenBrainz**: Real-time scrobbling and "Now Playing" status updates with automatic offline queueing.
* **Windows & Desktop Native**: Borderless true fullscreen, Windows taskbar DWM iconic thumbnail preview with playback controls, Discord Rich Presence integration, customizable global hotkeys, and minimize-to-system-tray.

## 📦 Downloads & Installation

### 🪟 Windows (x64)
* **Installer (`Looper-Setup-x64.exe`)**: Easy setup wizard that installs to local user programs (no admin rights needed), creates desktop/start-menu shortcuts, and associates common audio formats (`.mp3`, `.flac`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.aac`).
* **Portable (`looper-windows-x64-portable.zip`)**: Extract and launch `looper_player.exe` directly from any folder or USB drive with zero installation.

### 🐧 Linux (x86_64)
* **Debian / Ubuntu / Linux Mint**: Download and install `looper_1.0.0-beta.1_amd64.deb` (`sudo dpkg -i looper_*.deb`).
* **Fedora / RHEL / openSUSE**: Download and install `looper-1.0.0-beta.1.x86_64.rpm` (`sudo rpm -i looper-*.rpm`).
* **Universal AppImage**: Download `looper-1.0.0-beta.1-x86_64.AppImage`, run `chmod +x`, and execute anywhere.
* **Portable Tarball**: Download `looper-linux-x64.tar.gz` and extract anywhere.

> [!NOTE]  
> **Linux Prerequisite**: Ensure `libmpv` is installed on your distribution:
> - Ubuntu/Debian: `sudo apt install libmpv-dev libmpv2`
> - Arch Linux: `sudo pacman -S mpv`
> - Fedora: `sudo dnf install mpv-libs`
